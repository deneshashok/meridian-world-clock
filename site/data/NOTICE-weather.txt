Meridian — weather and place data notices

Weather
-------
Current conditions are fetched at runtime from Open-Meteo:
  https://open-meteo.com/

Open-Meteo data is provided by Open-Meteo.com contributors under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0):
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Weather conditions use the WMO 4677 interpretation code table supplied by
the API. No API key is required. Requests are made directly from the
visitor's browser; readings are cached locally for ten minutes.

Place coordinates
-----------------
City coordinates in places.js are derived from the GeoNames cities15000
and cities5000 datasets, with small curated coordinates for remote
stations and islands:
  https://www.geonames.org/

GeoNames data is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International licence (CC BY 4.0):
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
